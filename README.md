This is the official website of Battle For Whatever Thing (BFWT), which is an object show series I started in 2022 inspired by [Battle For Dream Island (BFDI)](https://bfdi.tv) made by Cary and Michael Huang.  
Both media are under the "object show" genre, but whereas BFDI is an animated web series, mine is a comic series drawn on books and passed by hand among my friends to read. I only started to upload the first 7(only 5, actually) seasons of my comics online since December of 2025. Now in 2026 after a 3 year hiatus BFWT is preparing to start its 8th season which will be drawn and shared online!
